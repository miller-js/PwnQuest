# PwnQuest — Competition Architecture & Operations

\## Overview

Designed and hosted \*\*PwnQuest\*\*, a hands-on King of the Hill (KoTH) penetration testing competition. Inspired by Hack The Box-style environments, the competition challenged participants to enumerate, exploit, compromise, and maintain control of vulnerable systems in a competitive environment.

As \*\*Competition Architect\*\*, I led the planning, development, infrastructure deployment, and execution of the competition, coordinating a 15-person Black Team and supporting 48 competitors throughout the event.

The competition was designed to provide an accessible but technically challenging experience for students of varying skill levels, combining offensive security challenges with real-world infrastructure and operational experience.

\---

\## My Role — Competition Architect

I oversaw the competition from initial planning through execution and post-event review.

\### Challenge Development

\- Led the development of \*\*20 King of the Hill challenges\*\* inspired by Hack The Box-style environments.

\- Personally authored and developed four challenges.

\- Coordinated developers, infrastructure, and scoring teams across a 15-person Black Team.

\- Tracked challenge development, deployment readiness, credentials, vulnerabilities, and testing progress.

\- Coordinated challenge integration and deployment to ensure consistency across the competition environment.

\### Infrastructure & Deployment

Designed and deployed the competition environment using RITSEC's OpenStack infrastructure.

\- Provisioned cloud resources for competition infrastructure.

\- Designed and deployed isolated networks, subnets, and segmented challenge environments.

\- Configured OpenStack security groups and network access rules.

\- Deployed challenge images and coordinated infrastructure integration.

\- Converted and prepared virtual machine images using QEMU, including QCOW and RAW formats.

\- Utilized the OpenStack CLI to manage cloud resources and image deployment.

\- Deployed and configured the competition scoring engine.

\- Coordinated VPN connectivity to provide remote access to competition infrastructure.

\### Competition Operations

Coordinated the operational aspects of the event for 48 competitors.

\- Managed registration, team organization, check-in, and attendance tracking.

\- Served as the primary point of contact for competitors and Black Team members.

\- Coordinated competition communications, schedules, and logistics with RITSEC.

\- Managed VPN access, scoring access, competition start times, and hands-on periods.

\- Troubleshot connectivity, virtualization, VPN, and environment issues throughout the event.

\- Assisted competitors across Windows, macOS, and Linux host environments.

\### Education & Competitor Support

Designed the competition to be approachable for beginners while still challenging experienced competitors.

\- Created a beginner preparation packet covering competition fundamentals and expectations.

\- Developed and delivered a penetration testing workshop to prepare participants.

\- Created Kali Linux virtual machine installation documentation for Windows users.

\- Created VPN installation and connectivity documentation.

\- Assisted competitors with virtualization and environment setup across different operating systems.

\- Provided hands-on guidance during the competition, covering enumeration, exploitation methodology, and challenge walkthroughs when needed.

\### Documentation & Organization

Created and maintained documentation throughout the development and execution process.

\- Competition packet containing rules, logistics, and participant information.

\- Kali Linux setup and installation guides.

\- VPN installation and troubleshooting guides.

\- OpenStack challenge image deployment documentation.

\- Challenge development and deployment tracking sheets.

\- Challenge inventory documenting challenge details, vulnerabilities, credentials, and development status.

\- Post-event documentation of lessons learned, deployment processes, and operational improvements.
